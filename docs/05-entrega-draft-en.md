## Project title
Flame in Freefall Explorer — AI insights from microgravity combustion data

## One-line summary
An interactive dashboard that ranks and explains NASA microgravity combustion experiments, with an AI assistant that answers only from cited data, to support fire safety on Moon and Mars missions.

## Project description (structure)
- **Problem:** Decades of microgravity combustion results are hard to compare and apply to spacecraft fire safety.
- **Solution:** We unify results from [N] NASA experiments into a common schema, rank conditions by explainable fire-risk criteria, and let users ask questions answered with citations.
- **How it works:** Data → schema → ranking → AI assistant → dashboard.
- **Impact:** Helps mission planners and researchers spot risky materials and cabin conditions faster.
- **Limitations:** Small sample ([N] experiments); exploratory, not official NASA guidance.
- **What's next:** More experiments, uncertainty modelling, validation with domain experts.
